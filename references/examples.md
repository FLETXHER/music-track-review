# Calibration Examples

These are behavioral calibration examples, not immutable genre truth. The important parts are score separation, style specificity, listening-era reasoning, and a concise diagnostic QC note. Each score is based on a complete internal five-dimension check; the final wording keeps only score-moving evidence rather than becoming a dimension-by-dimension report.
