# Calibration Examples

These are behavioral calibration examples, not immutable genre truth. The important parts are the score separation, style specificity, listening-era reasoning, and compact note. Each note compresses its internal five-dimension coverage into grouped clauses rather than a dimension-by-dimension report.

## JAY-Z — What More Can I Say

```text
Genre：Hip Hop
Style：East Coast Rap / Hardcore Hip-Hop / Soul
听感年代：现代
大众性：3/5
音乐性：5/5

备注：大众性3分：反复句和鼓点提供入口，采样编排与音色易读；长篇verse、戏剧化混音和弱副歌削弱结构回归。音乐性5分：Flow与采样编配推进自然，制作动态、说唱控制清晰；戏剧张力随段落累积并形成统一表达。亮点：Flow重音与采样动态的联动。
```
