# Genre Family Guide

Use these broad families as orientation. They are deliberately broad and are **not** an exhaustive ontology.

- Blues
- Brass & Military
- Children's
- Classical
- Electronic
- Folk / World & Country
- Funk / Soul
- Hip Hop
- Jazz
- Latin
- Non-Music
- Pop
- Reggae
- Rock
- Stage & Screen

These families mirror a common public database convention. They are internal research orientation only; v0.8 does not output a separate Genre field. A hybrid track may draw from multiple families without requiring multiple final Style labels.

Do not let the family list restrict Style vocabulary. Fine styles can be much more specific, and public tagging systems evolve over time.
