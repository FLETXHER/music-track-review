# Genre Family Guide

Use these broad families as orientation. They are deliberately broad and are **not** an exhaustive ontology.

- Blues
- Brass & Military
- Children's
- Classical
- Electronic
- Folk / World & Country
- Funk / Soul
- Hip Hop
- Jazz
- Latin
- Non-Music
- Pop
- Reggae
- Rock
- Stage & Screen

These families mirror a common public database convention. Multiple Genres may be used when a track genuinely spans major musical families.

Do not let the family list restrict Style vocabulary. Fine styles can be much more specific, and public tagging systems evolve over time.
