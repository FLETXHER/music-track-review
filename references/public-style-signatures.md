# Public Style Signatures — heuristic listening questions (v0.1)

Use as **listening questions**, not as rigid thresholds. The musical identity of the **exact recording** matters more than a generic tag on a page. These project-supplied questions are heuristic aids, not evidence for any recording and not an official taxonomy. Confirm musical traits from the exact recording and credible sources.

| Candidate Style or warning | Ask what is actually audible | Common false positive to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Ambient | Is textural continuity/atmosphere the organizing idea, with pulse or tune possibly secondary? | Any quiet song with reverb |
| Modern Classical | Are contemporary concert-music or chamber compositional practices central? | Any piano piece from a contemporary game |
| Chamber Music | Is the arrangement structured around interacting small-ensemble parts? | Any strings in a soundtrack |
| Minimalism | Are repetition and gradual patterned changes compositionally structural? | A simple repeated hook |
| Chiptune | Are chip-synth/game-console-like synthesis practices central to the sound? | Any video-game soundtrack |
| Soundtrack | Is the recording demonstrably used as screen/game music? This is *context only*. | Mistaking source context for a sonic genre |
| Video Game Music | Is it actually tied to a game score? This is *context only*. | Automatically assigning orchestral/electronic style |
| Instrumental | Does it lack a continuous independent lyrical/lead-vocal expression? This is *form only*. | Treating a vocal chop as a complete lead vocal |
| Acoustic | Are the sound sources presented acoustically rather than primarily electronically? This is *form/production only*. | Assuming acoustic implies Folk |
| House | Does a repeated four-beat dance pulse and house-derived groove define the arrangement? | Any 4/4 kick pattern |
| Techno | Is the motoric electronic pattern and timbral/structural evolution more central than pop songwriting? | Any instrumental dance recording |
| Hard Techno | Is hard, forceful techno-oriented percussion and its driving functional structure dominant? | Loudness or distortion alone |
| Trance | Are sustained harmonic arpeggios/synth buildups and hypnotic melodic evolution central? | Any extended electronic track |
| UK Garage | Do syncopated/shuffling UKG rhythmic practices characterize the groove? | Every song with a shuffled snare |
| Drum n Bass | Is a fast broken-beat bass-music rhythmic identity the core? | Tempo alone without DnB drum language |
| Jungle | Are chopped breakbeats and bass-focused jungle patterning central? | Any breakbeat loop |
| Jersey Club | Are its signature kick-pattern and stuttering club rhythms material? | Any chopped vocals over dance drums |
| Breakcore | Is aggressive/complex breakbeat manipulation genuinely central? | Fast drums in general |
| Hyperpop | Does hyper-digital pop production and pop-song structure define the result? | Every distorted or experimental electronic track |
| Synth-pop | Is synth-centered pop songwriting with recognizable hooks central? | The presence of a single synth |
| Alt-Pop | Does a pop songwriting core use distinctly nonstandard textures or forms? | Artist branding as 'alternative' |
| Contemporary R&B | Is contemporary R&B phrasing/groove/harmony central? | Any smooth or soft pop ballad |
| Neo Soul | Does a soul-rooted rhythmic, harmonic and vocal approach define the recording? | Any slow R&B |
| City Pop | Are city-pop-specific pop/jazz/funk arrangement practices central? | Any Japanese-language pop |
| Boom Bap | Is a sample-oriented, pronounced kick/snare hip-hop groove central? | Any older East Coast rap song |
| Trap | Are recognizable trap drum programming and bass idiom central? | Any rap with 808 bass |
| Drill | Do drill's characteristic rhythmic/bass idioms actually lead the track? | Any dark/aggressive street rap |
| Pop Rap | Is the rap structure substantially integrated with a pop-hook/songwriting framework? | Any melodic rap |
| Jazzy Hip-Hop | Is jazz-derived harmonic/arrangement language more than incidental sampling? | A single jazz sample |
| Indie Rock | Are indie-rock band practices and guitar/band arrangement central? | The artist is signed to an indie label |
| Art Rock | Are exploratory compositional/arrangement choices meaningfully part of a rock idiom? | Any long rock song |
| Post-Punk | Is a post-punk rhythm/timbre/band idiom central? | Every bass-forward guitar song |
| Shoegaze | Are dense processed guitar textures a major organizing element? | Any guitar with reverb |
| Dream Pop | Are dreamy textures embedded in recognizable pop songwriting? | Any slow ambient track |
| Orchestral contextual warning | `Orchestra` is an ensemble descriptor, not proof a film/game cue is 'orchestral pop' or contemporary classical. | Genre invented from instrumentation alone |

## Candidate selection / checking

1. First describe the audible rhythmic, melodic, harmonic and production evidence without writing style names.
2. Propose 1–3 supported established terms from `public-style-seed-v0.1.json`, or other established public terms when the seed is incomplete.
3. Verify one **primary musical identity**, not only form/context attributes.
4. Keep material soundtrack and instrumental indicators; avoid generic tag inflation.
5. If no strong primary style emerges, mark the song for further listening or source verification rather than inserting a convenient generic genre.

**Not a private label mapper.** The listed terms and signatures do not establish that a private labeling UI accepts the proposed wording. No numeric accuracy or coverage is claimed.
