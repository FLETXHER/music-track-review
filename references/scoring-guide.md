# Scoring Guide

Use this reference to decide the two numeric scores. The two scores answer different questions and must not be coupled.

## Mainstream Appeal

Mainstream Appeal estimates how immediately accessible the track is to a broad contemporary listener. It is not a proxy for fame, chart history, artistic value, or personal taste.

Use five dimensions as an internal checklist. Their qualitative priorities guide attention only: do not turn them into separate numeric subscores or a weighted arithmetic result.

| Dimension | Priority | Higher appeal | Lower appeal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Melody / hook | High | memorable, singable, clear hook or refrain | unusual intervals, weak or absent hook, difficult-to-follow melodic writing |
| Rhythm / groove | High | predictable pulse, stable groove, easy bodily entrainment | highly fragmented rhythm, frequent meter disruption, hard-to-locate pulse |
| Arrangement accessibility | Medium | clear texture, familiar harmonic movement, readable sections | dense/empty texture, frequent modulation, unusually complex harmonic language |
| Timbre / mix accessibility | Medium | rounded/familiar timbres, clear vocal focus, comfortable balance | abrasive/noisy/industrial timbres, extreme vocal processing, deliberately difficult sonics |
| Structural familiarity | Supporting | recognizable sections and expected returns | linear/non-repeating form, extreme length, interlude-like or fragmentary structure |

### Mainstream calibration

- **5/5** — unusually immediate and accessible across melody, rhythm, sonics, and structure. The note must name several concrete accessibility strengths rather than only saying that broad-listener friction is low.
- **4/5** — clearly accessible and polished, with one or two meaningful barriers or less-conventional choices.
- **3/5** — broadly understandable but has obvious limits: weak traditional hook, long/slow form, niche vocal delivery, unusual production, or reduced pop structure.
- **2/5** — strong niche/experimental/functional identity; conventional melodic and structural access is limited.
- **1/5** — extremely inaccessible for broad listening or dominated by severe sonic/musical problems.

A track can be musically excellent and still score 2–3 for Mainstream Appeal.

### Boundary calibration

- Do not award 4/5 merely because a track is polished, famous, critically acclaimed, or easy to enjoy.
- 3/5 is appropriate when a track is understandable and professionally produced but has clear broad-listener barriers such as:
  - long verse-led form;
  - weak or absent melodic chorus;
  - niche vocal delivery;
  - club-functional repetition;
  - unusual or abrasive production;
  - reduced conventional pop structure.
- Slow tempo alone is not a reason to reduce a highly accessible pop song from 5/5 to 4/5.
- A track can still be 5/5 Mainstream Appeal when melody, hook, structure, vocal presentation, and sonics are highly accessible despite being slow, atmospheric, or understated.
- For strongly functional club music with little conventional melodic/song-form access, 2/5 may be more appropriate than 3/5 even when the production is highly professional.

The following anchors are boundary references, not fixed song answers to memorize:

- JAY-Z — What More Can I Say:
  Mainstream Appeal 3/5 is the anchor because the track is long and rap-verse-led with limited traditional melodic-chorus emphasis, despite polished and dramatic production.

- Joji — Die For You:
  Mainstream Appeal 5/5 is the anchor because melody, chorus, structure, vocal presentation, and production are all highly accessible; slow/dreamy presentation alone should not reduce it to 4.

- Boys Noize — Sireneh:
  Mainstream Appeal 2/5 is the anchor for highly functional Hard Techno whose primary identity is sustained club energy rather than conventional melody, chorus, or pop song structure.

## Musicality

Musicality estimates the quality and effectiveness of the musical result, independent of popularity.

Use five internal dimensions:

1. **Melody / flow**
   - memorable or expressive melodic writing;
   - natural contour and tension;
   - effective hook/refrain when relevant;
   - in rap or other non-melodic-forward music, evaluate flow design and prosodic control instead of forcing a pop-melody standard.

2. **Arrangement / instrumentation**
   - coherent instrumentation;
   - clear section contrast and progression;
   - details that meaningfully develop the track;
   - strong coordination among melody, rhythm, texture, and transitions.

3. **Production / mix**
   - convincing dynamics and tonal balance;
   - separation, spatial placement, and texture appropriate to the style;
   - distinctive but intentional sound design;
   - production choices that support the composition rather than merely sound expensive.

4. **Vocal / performance**
   - distinctive tone or delivery;
   - technical control appropriate to the style;
   - emotional fit and interaction with the arrangement;
   - harmonies or layered vocals when relevant.
   - Reduce the weight of conventional singing criteria for rap, instrumental-forward electronic music, spoken word, and related forms.

5. **Emotion / expression**
   - clear emotional direction;
   - convincing energy, atmosphere, or narrative;
   - meaningful progression or contrast;
   - coherent relationship between performance, composition, and production.

### Musicality calibration

- **5/5** — rare and exceptional. It has a stable professional baseline, no meaningful musical or technical deduction, and clear affirmative highlights across multiple core dimensions that reinforce one another. The note must explain what moves it from 4/5 to 5/5; fame or acclaim is irrelevant.
- **4/5** — the normal professional pass band: mature, reliable, coherent, and effective. The overall result is stably professional across most relevant core dimensions; minor limitations remain compatible with 4/5. It can have a modest arrangement, a conventional style, restrained execution, or minor limitations and remain 4/5. Lack of novelty, elite vocals, advanced harmony, or a showpiece highlight is not by itself a reason for 3/5.
- **3/5** — a legitimate borderline or discard-tendency result, but not an automatic discard. It requires a clear pattern of meaningful foundational weakness that materially prevents a stable professional result. Do not use 3/5 merely because a track is not exceptional, has a simple genre language, a restrained arrangement, ordinary writing, or no obvious route to 5/5.
- **2/5** — multiple fundamental weaknesses leave the result below a stable professional pass line.
- **1/5** — severe musical or technical defects, obvious non-professional failure, or material too compromised to assess normally.

### Minor limitation versus foundational weakness

A minor limitation is a bounded tradeoff that does not break a core dimension: for example, a restrained but coherent arrangement, conventional writing, a limited amount of contrast, or an ordinary but controlled performance. It can prevent 5/5 without forcing 3/5.

A foundational weakness materially impairs a core result across the track or at a central moment: for example, unstable flow or melody, ineffective section development, a mix that repeatedly obscures the music, unreliable performance control, or emotion that never coheres with the writing and production. Use 3/5 only when a clear, meaningful pattern of foundational weakness materially prevents a stable professional result; do not count dimensions mechanically.

### Affirmative evidence for 5/5

A top score requires positive evidence, not merely the absence of a flaw.

- **Mainstream Appeal 5/5** — name several concrete reasons the hook, groove, section design, vocal presentation, and/or timbre are highly accessible.
- **Musicality 5/5** — name actual standout evidence such as memorable melodic tension or flow control, meaningful harmonic/arrangement development, a coherent sonic identity, dynamics and mix that strengthen the track, distinctive and controlled performance, or unusually unified emotion and expression.

Do not impose a quota of features. The evidence must be specific to the recording and sufficient to explain why 4/5 is not the better result.

## Score-note requirement

A score note is part of the scoring decision, not merely a description of the track. It must show both the evidence that supports the score and the evidence that limits it when a higher band is possible.

### Five-dimension coverage

The default note must cover all five relevant dimensions for each numeric score. This is a content-coverage gate, not a five-subscore table, an arithmetic formula, or a reason to count minor limitations mechanically.

- **Mainstream Appeal** — make a concrete judgement about melody / hook, rhythm / groove, arrangement accessibility, timbre / mix accessibility, and structural familiarity.
- **Musicality** — make a concrete judgement about melody / flow, arrangement / instrumentation, production / mix, vocal / performance, and emotion / expression.
- For rap, spoken word, instrumental, instrumental-forward electronic music, and similar forms, handle the vocal / performance dimension as form-appropriate performance or execution. Do not fabricate conventional vocal evidence.

Use compact natural-language clauses rather than printed dimension headings, pass/fail labels, or internal scores. Each score section must show what supports the selected band and, where a higher band is possible, what limits it.

### Score-to-note consistency check

Before final output:

1. choose the score candidate;
2. write the five-dimension evidence, including supports and limitations;
3. compare the documented strengths, barriers, and foundational weaknesses with the chosen score;
4. revise the note or score if they materially conflict.

Do not leave a note that describes several broad-listener barriers while claiming Mainstream Appeal 5/5, or a note that describes a stable professional result with only minor limitations while mechanically reducing Musicality to 3/5.

### Positive highlights and tone

Include 1–2 concrete, recording-specific positive highlights inside the same note. A highlight may concern a hook, flow, rhythmic design, arrangement, instrumentation, sound design, mix or dynamics, vocal control, emotional progression, harmonic movement, or a section transition. Do not create a standalone highlight field.

The note must use a neutral, professional, diagnostic work-note tone. Do not use emoji, emoticons, memes, jokes, exaggerated exclamations, slang-heavy or overly personalized language, rhetorical questions, or first-person preference. Vague praise such as “很好听”, “很高级”, “很炸”, “很有感觉”, or “完成度高” is not a highlight unless immediately tied to a concrete musical reason.

- **5/5** — state concrete affirmative evidence for the top band and why 4/5 is not enough; do not use “没有明显问题” as the reason.
- **4/5** — state the main strengths and at least one specific reason it is not 5/5. A minor limitation is sufficient here and does not make the track a 3/5.
- **3/5** — state at least one genuine strength or point of entry, then the foundational weakness pattern that prevents 4/5.
- **2/5** — state the main barrier, limitation, or weakness that prevents 3/5.
- **1/5** — state the severe failure point.

A note that only describes what the track contains is invalid. It must explain which factors raised the score and which factors limited it. Generic praise such as “表现很好”, “完成度很高”, “很专业”, “很有特色”, “很不错”, or “整体优秀” is invalid unless immediately followed by concrete audible or researched evidence.

Do not substitute release background, single status, artist fame, charts, sales, awards, cultural status, album background, or credits themselves for a deduction reason. Credits, producers, or instruments may appear only when they explain a specific musical result.

Good:
- `大众性3分：反复句提供旋律入口，鼓点律动稳定；采样层次可读，音色整体易接受；长篇verse推进和弱旋律型副歌削弱结构回归感。音乐性4分：Flow与旋律重音自然，编配与制作层次连贯；说唱控制稳定，情绪推进清楚，但段落发展仍较克制。亮点：Flow控制与采样层次的配合。`

Invalid:
- `大众性3分：歌曲使用Soul采样，由某制作人制作；音乐性4分：歌手表现很好。`

## How to write the note

The note should be short but diagnostic.

Good:
- `大众性4分：旋律与Hook清楚，groove稳定；编配层次易读，音色平衡自然；段落回归明确，但副歌推进仍有限。音乐性4分：旋律或Flow连贯，配器与制作服务段落；表演控制稳定，情绪表达完整，但发展幅度不足以到5分。亮点：Hook与段落推进的衔接。`

Weak:
- `大众性一般；音乐性不错。`

Cover every relevant dimension with concise clauses. Do not turn the note into a scorecard; use the strongest concrete evidence within each dimension and keep the result readable.
